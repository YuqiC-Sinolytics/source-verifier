<!-- title: Obesity drugs -->
GLP-1 receptor agonists have reshaped obesity treatment faster than any drug class in recent memory.

In the STEP 1 trial, semaglutide produced an [average weight loss of 14.9% over 68 weeks](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183), a result no earlier pharmacological therapy had come close to.

More than [40% of US adults](https://www.cdc.gov/obesity/data/adult.html) now meet the clinical definition of obesity, which is what makes the addressable market so unusually large.

Tirzepatide went further still, [outperforming semaglutide with 26% average weight loss](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736-24-01234/fulltext) in head-to-head trials.

Both drugs work by [mimicking a gut hormone that regulates appetite](https://www.nature.com/nrendo/) and slows gastric emptying.

The commercial consequences were immediate: by late 2023 Novo Nordisk's [market value briefly exceeded the entire GDP of Denmark](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/novo-nordisk-market-value-denmark-gdp-2023-09-01/).
