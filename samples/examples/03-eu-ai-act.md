<!-- title: EU AI Act -->
The EU AI Act is the first comprehensive statutory framework for artificial intelligence anywhere in the world.

It [entered into force on 1 August 2024](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj), with obligations phasing in over the three years that follow.

Penalties are steep: infringements of the prohibited-practices rules carry fines of [up to 6% of global annual turnover](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai).

Obligations for general-purpose AI models [began to apply on 2 August 2025](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/gpai-timeline-2025/), a year after entry into force.

The regime is extraterritorial: it [applies to providers established outside the EU](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence) whenever the output of their system is used inside the Union.

Systems in the high-risk tier must [undergo conformity assessment before being placed on the market](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/high-level-summary/), which is the single largest compliance cost for most vendors.

A [2025 industry survey](https://ai-governance-institute.eu/2025-compliance-survey) found that 61% of EU firms had already begun compliance work.
